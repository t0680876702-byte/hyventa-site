Cookie Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Hyventa ("we", "us", "our") uses cookies and similar

technologies on our website. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the

site work, remember your preferences, and understand how the site is used. Similar

technologies include local storage and pixels.

2. Types of cookies we use

CategoryPurposeConsent required?
EssentialCore site functions: security, load balancing, remembering your language and cookie choices, running the on-site AI assistant widgetNo (strictly necessary)
AnalyticsUnderstand traffic and improve the site (e.g. pages visited, aggregated statistics)Yes
FunctionalRemember preferences and enhance featuresYes
MarketingMeasure campaigns and show relevant ads (only if enabled)Yes

We do not use non-essential cookies until you give consent via the cookie banner.

3. The on-site AI assistant

Our website assistant (the chat widget) uses essential storage to keep your conversation

session working. It does not use your data to train AI models. See the Privacy Policy for how

conversation data is processed.

4. Third-party cookies

Some cookies may be set by third-party services we use (e.g. analytics providers, or Google

where embedded content is used). These providers set cookies under their own policies.

5. Managing your choices

  • - Use the cookie banner on our site to accept or reject non-essential cookies, and to

change your choice at any time.

  • - You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies

may break parts of the site.

6. Changes

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date reflects the latest version.

7. Contact

Questions about cookies: privacy@hyventa.ai

Las cookies son pequeños archivos de texto que se guardan en tu dispositivo al visitar un

cómo se usa. Tecnologías similares incluyen el almacenamiento local y los píxeles.

Файли cookie — це невеликі текстові файли, що зберігаються на вашому пристрої під час

дають зрозуміти, як використовується сайт. До подібних технологій належать локальне сховище

та пікселі.
