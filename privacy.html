Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

Hyventa ("we", "us", "our") provides AI-powered virtual assistants ("AI Employees")

that businesses embed on their websites and messaging channels. This Privacy Policy

explains what personal data we process, why, and your rights under the GDPR and

applicable law.

1. Who we are (Data Controller)

Hyventa — sole proprietor (ФОП), registered in Ukraine.

Contact: privacy@hyventa.ai

For personal data that our clients' end-users submit to an AI Employee, our client is

the Data Controller and Hyventa acts as a Data Processor on their behalf.

2. What data we process

  • - Contact & lead data you provide to an assistant: name, phone, email, message,

and any details you choose to share (e.g. your request or interest).

  • - Conversation content: the text of your chat with the assistant.
  • - Technical data: IP address, browser type, timestamps, cookies (see §8).

We ask you not to share sensitive data (passwords, card numbers, ID/passport,

health or financial details). Our assistants are instructed not to request such data.

3. Why we process it (purposes & legal basis)

PurposeLegal basis (GDPR Art. 6)
Answer your questions, provide the servicePerformance of a contract / legitimate interest
Follow up on your request or leadConsent / legitimate interest
Improve and secure our serviceLegitimate interest
Comply with legal obligationsLegal obligation

4. How the AI works & third parties (Sub-processors)

To generate responses, the text of your conversation is sent to trusted AI providers.

**These providers do not use your data to train their models when accessed via their API,

and data is retained only briefly for abuse-prevention before deletion.**

Our sub-processors:

ProviderPurposeRegion
OpenAI, L.L.C.Language model (assistant brain)USA
PiAPIImage/video/music generationUSA / Asia
OpenRouterLLM routingUSA

n8n GmbH (n8n Cloud)Orchestration & data storageEU
Google (Drive/Workspace)File storageEU / USA

Transfers outside the EU are covered by Standard Contractual Clauses (SCC).

Your contact and lead data are stored within our n8n environment — not on the

AI providers' servers.

5. Data retention

We keep your data only as long as necessary for the purposes above, then delete or

anonymise it. Leads are kept for the duration of the client relationship unless you

request deletion earlier.

6. Your rights (GDPR)

You have the right to access, rectify, erase ("right to be forgotten"), restrict or

object to processing, and data portability. To exercise any right, email

privacy@hyventa.ai. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection

authority.

7. Security

We use access controls, encryption in transit, and the principle of data minimisation.

For clients in sensitive sectors (healthcare, legal, finance) we offer a PRO option with

EU-region or self-hosted AI, where data does not leave the designated infrastructure.

8. Cookies

We use essential cookies (site function) and, with your consent, analytics cookies.

You can manage preferences via the cookie banner.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date reflects the latest version.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: privacy@hyventa.ai
